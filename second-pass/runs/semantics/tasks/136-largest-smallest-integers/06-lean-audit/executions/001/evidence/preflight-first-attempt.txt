DIAGNOSIS
The sandbox reports a namespace PID from getpid(), but its /proc mount has no
/proc/<namespace-pid>/exe. Lean 4.22 calls readlink on that numeric path to
locate its installation. See lean-environment-workaround.txt. The successful
rerun used proc-self-readlink.c, which maps only /proc/<digits>/exe to the
equivalent /proc/self/exe path.
