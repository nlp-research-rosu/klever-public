The audit container exposes /proc/self/exe but not /proc/<getpid()>/exe.
Lean 4.22.0's IO.appPath uses the latter and initially failed with:
  error: failed to locate application

The audit-local LD_PRELOAD shim in /tmp/audit-work/libproc-self-exe.so maps
/proc/<pid>/exe readlink calls to /proc/self/exe. (The PID namespace can make
the numeric path either absent or refer to an unrelated visible process.) The copied
toolchain launchers under /tmp/audit-work/lean-overlay/bin remain linked to the
pinned /opt/elan/toolchains/leanprover--lean4---v4.22.0 libraries. With this
container compatibility shim, `lean --version` reports commit
ba2cbbf09d4978f416e0ebd1fceeebc2c4138c05 and Lake reports Lean 4.22.0.
