The sandbox returned inner PID 3 from getpid(), but /proc exposed outer PID
1259. Lean 4.22 resolves its prefix with /proc/<getpid>/exe, so the mismatch
caused the pre-project failure. The narrowly scoped shim in
00-lean-proc-pid-shim.c returns the outer Pid field only.
